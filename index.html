    ₹Old or new regime?

    Rough estimate for residents under 60, including 4% cess, without surcharge or interest. Deductions shown under Tax saving are used for the old regime. Your CA or the portal's own calculation is final.

    →On the portal
    1. Log in at incometax.gov.in → e-File → Income Tax Returns → File Income Tax Return.
    2. Choose the assessment year, Online, then Individual, then ITR-3.
    3. Check the pre-filled salary, interest, dividends and TDS against your AIS. Fix any mismatch.
    4. Fill each schedule with the numbers above, in the same order.
    5. Choose your regime in the General Information part. Pick the one shown as lower above if you're allowed to.
    6. Pay any tax due (Challan 280 through e-Pay Tax), add the challan, then submit and e-verify with Aadhaar OTP within 30 days.

    Capital gains

    Equity shares and equity funds: held up to 12 months is short-term, taxed at 20%. Held more than 12 months is long-term, taxed at 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh a year. Debt funds bought after 1 April 2023 are taxed at your slab rate. Estimate before 4% cess and surcharge, using first-in-first-out. Check with your CA before filing.

    Import from Excel

    Upload an Excel or CSV file. The app finds the Date, Amount, Category and other columns by itself. Bank statements with Debit and Credit columns work too. For best results, use the template.

    Adding 5 to 10 years of old data

    You don't need to type it by hand. Download statements, import them one year at a time, and only add full detail where it matters. Tap a step to open it.

    1Set starting balances

    Decide your start date, for example 1 April 2016. Under Accounts, add each bank, cash and card account with its balance on that date. The app then works out every later balance from your entries.

    After importing, compare the app's balance with your real bank balance. If they match, your history is complete.

    2Import bank statements
    1. In net banking, download each account's statement as Excel or CSV, one financial year (April to March) at a time.
    2. Tap Choose Excel file above and pick the statement. The app finds the Date, Debit, Credit and Description columns itself.
    3. It guesses categories (Swiggy → Food, Salary → Salary, Amazon → Shopping) and shows a preview. Tap Import.
    4. Repeat for each year. Entries already in the app are skipped, so overlapping statements are safe.

    Transfers between your own accounts and credit-card bill payments appear in statements too. Delete or recategorise them in History so they aren't counted as spending.

    3Import investments
    InvestmentWhere to get the history
    StocksBroker tradebook, e.g. Zerodha Console → Reports → Tradebook
    Mutual fundsCAS statement from CAMS, KFintech or MF Central
    PPF, SukanyaPassbook: one entry per year is enough
    Insurance, ULIPPremium receipts: one entry per premium
    FD, NPSBank or NPS statement: one entry per deposit

    Tap Download template above, copy the broker's columns into it (Date, Investment Type, Name, Action, Quantity, Price), and import it.

    Enter every buy of shares and funds you still hold or sold recently. Capital gains and the ITR helper need the buy date and price.

    4Summarise old expenses

    Daily details from years ago add little value. For older years, use the template and enter:

    • One row per category per month, e.g. 30-06-2019 · Expense · Food · ₹6,200. In Excel, drag to fill the dates quickly.
    • Or one row per category per year if you only want the long-term trend.
    • Salary, dividends and interest: monthly or yearly totals are enough.

    Keep full, day-by-day detail for the last one or two years.
